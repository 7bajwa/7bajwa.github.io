FAQ

Questions artists ask first.

Why not just watch YouTube tutorials?

Tutorials teach a technique. They cannot look at your portfolio. I make tutorials too, on YouTube and Topmate, and they are great once you know what to learn. Mentorship tells you what your portfolio is missing and in what order to fix it. As Vikram put it after his session: "Unlike YouTube tutorials it provides you with a structured path to learn."

What exactly do I get in one session?

A 1-on-1 call focused on what you need: portfolio review and an actionable roadmap, career direction and job strategy, resume, LinkedIn and cover letter, environment and foliage technique, or UE5 and Blender workflow. You leave knowing what to work on next.

How much does it cost?

The portfolio readiness assessment is free. A written readiness report is $9, an expert review is $29, and 1-on-1 mentorship runs from $99 to $599 for mentorship package. See pricing. All payments go through Topmate.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that. What I can give you is a hard, honest look at your work against what studios actually look for, Skills they need from you and a clear plan to close the gap.

I am a student in India - is this affordable?

Yes. Artists in India: we can offer a discount. Fill the form below with the request and we will sort it out. You can also start with the free portfolio readiness check, which costs nothing.

About

I am a Senior Biome and Foliage Artist with 10+ years building photorealistic environments for the industry's biggest titles. Rockstar Games on GTA 6, Gunzilla's Off the Grid, Hangar 13's Mafia, and Forza Horizon 4.

I specialise in making environments feel alive. Foliage systems, biome pipelines, vegetation at scale. I also build the tools and scripts that make production sustainable.

I run Future/Past Academy, have spoken at BCon 2023 to 2025 as keynote, and lectured at university level on foliage art and game pipelines.

I also write poetry, collect coins from 50+ countries, and cook anything I can get my hands on.
